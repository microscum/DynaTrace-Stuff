# Dynatrace Dashboards Flashcards

## Flashcards for DynaTrace Associate Certification

### Q: What is a Dynatrace dashboard?
A: A customizable page that aggregates tiles and visual visualizations to display metrics, problems, logs, traces, and entity data for monitoring and reporting.

### Q: What is a dashboard tile?
A: A widget on a dashboard that shows a chart, single-value metric, table, problem feed, or custom content based on Dynatrace data sources.

### Q: Why are dashboards important for DynaTrace Associate certification?
A: They demonstrate how to present operational health, business metrics, and problem trends in an easily consumable way for stakeholders and monitoring teams.

### Q: What are common data sources used in Dynatrace dashboards?
A: Metrics, problems, logs & events, traces, and entities.

### Q: What is the difference between a standard dashboard and a custom dashboard?
A: Standard dashboards are prebuilt or shared templates, while custom dashboards are created and configured by users for specific monitoring needs.

### Q: How do dashboard filters and selectors help in Dynatrace?
A: They narrow the displayed data to specific services, hosts, tags, environments, or timeframes so dashboards show only relevant information.

### Q: What is one best practice for dashboard design?
A: Keep dashboards focused by limiting tiles to the most important metrics and using consistent naming and layout for shared views.

### Q: What visualization types are commonly used on dashboards?
A: Charts, heatmaps, data tables, single-value tiles, and custom query widgets.

### Q: How can dashboards support collaboration and reporting?
A: By sharing dashboards with teams, setting permissions, exporting views as PDF/PNG, and using shared templates.

### Q: What is the role of dashboards in incident management?
A: Dashboards provide quick situational awareness, help track SLA and availability, and support root-cause validation during investigations.

### Q: What does a dashboard library contain?
A: A collection of predefined dashboards, saved dashboards, and shared templates for reuse across teams.

### Q: How should you choose dashboard content for different audiences?
A: Use operational metrics and problem summaries for SREs, and high-level availability or business KPIs for stakeholders.
